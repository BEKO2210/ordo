Privacy policy

In short

Ordo collects no data about you. The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your files stay on your device. Ordo never connects to the internet on its own. The only exceptions: if you set up AI commands, Ordo downloads the language model once, and Ordo asks Google Play whether you bought the AI unlock.

Permissions

Ordo only asks for what a file manager needs:

  • Access to all files: show, copy, move, rename and delete files.
  • See installed apps: show the app overview and open files with the right app.
  • Install and remove apps: only when you start it in Ordo.
  • Internet and Wi-Fi state: only for servers you set up, for the built-in FTP server and for the one-time download of the AI model.
  • Google Play billing: only for the one-time AI unlock.
  • Notifications and background service: long tasks keep running and show their progress.
  • Fingerprint or screen lock: Android does the check. Ordo only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Shortcuts: only when you add one to your home screen.

Data on your device

Ordo stores data only on your device: your settings, bookmarks, the sign-in details of servers you set up and whether the AI is unlocked. Deleted files stay in the folder “.OrdoTrash” on the same storage, usually for 30 days.

Ordo sends none of this to me or to anyone else. Ordo is excluded from Android backup: settings, sign-in details and the vault never go to your Google account or to a new phone during a transfer.

When you uninstall Ordo, Android deletes the app data on the device.

Vault

Ordo encrypts files in the vault with AES-256 and keeps them in the app's protected storage. The key stays in your device's keystore and never leaves it.

Important: If you uninstall Ordo or clear its app data, the files in the vault are lost. Take them out or back them up first.

Backup

A backup is a ZIP file in the place you choose, for example on a USB drive. If you want, Ordo encrypts it with your password (AES-256). Ordo does not store the password.

If you choose cloud storage such as Google Drive, that provider's app transfers the file. The provider's privacy policy applies.

Your own servers

When you set up a server (FTP, SFTP, SMB or WebDAV), Ordo connects to it directly. The server's operator sees your IP address and the files you transfer. Nobody else is involved.

The built-in FTP server only runs while you have it switched on.

AI commands

AI commands are off until you set them up. Ordo then downloads the language model Qwen3.5-4B (about 2.6 GB) once from Hugging Face (Hugging Face, Inc., USA). Hugging Face sees your IP address to deliver the file (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): https://huggingface.co/privacy

After that the AI runs only on your device. Your commands and file names never leave it. You can remove the model in the AI sheet at any time.

Google Play

When you download Ordo from Google Play, Google processes data under its own rules: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google only gives me summarised figures, such as installs and crash reports if you allowed them in Android. I cannot identify anyone from them.

The AI commands are a one-time purchase. Google Play handles the payment under its own rules; Ordo never sees your payment details. Ordo only asks Google Play whether this Google account bought the AI unlock and stores a single “unlocked” flag on your device. For each purchase, Google gives me the order details I need for my accounts and for refunds (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).

Website

When you open it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub also processes data in the USA and relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other providers.

More: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Contact by email

When you email me, I process your address and your message in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete them once your request is settled. My email provider is Proton AG in Switzerland.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Email me to use them.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The one responsible for me is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg: https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de

Apart from the order details Google gives me about purchases, I hold no data about you from the app.
